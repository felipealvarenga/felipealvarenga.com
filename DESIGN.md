# Design

## Theme
Dark primary (near-black tinted toward cold blue, never #000), light variant for prefers-color-scheme: light. Restrained color strategy: tinted neutrals, links in the text color with an underline, hover rows get a faint surface tint. No accent color.

## Typography
Geist (Google Fonts) for everything; Geist Mono for years and small meta. Body 1rem/1.6, intro 1.25rem, headings 0.875rem uppercase tracked labels or plain 1rem weight 500. Scale ratio 1.25.

## Layout
Single column, max-width 42rem, left aligned, page padding clamp(1rem, 4vw, 2rem). Rows: grid [4rem 1fr auto], hairline separators oklch 25%.

## Components
Nav (name left, links right), intro paragraph, row lists (Writing, Work), press note, footer links. Hover: row background oklch(20%) and link underline. Focus: 2px outline offset 2px.
