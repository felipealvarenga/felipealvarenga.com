# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Journalists, podcast hosts, hiring managers, visa officers and engineers who land on felipealvarenga.com from LinkedIn, an email signature or a byline. They arrive on a laptop or phone, give the page ten seconds, and want to know who Felipe is, what he has built, and how to reach him.

## Product Purpose
A one-page personal site and press kit. It supports an O-1A/EB-1A evidence record: it must read as the site of a senior engineer at the edge of applied AI in finance, with a public voice, list his writing as it gets published, and make contact trivial. Success: a journalist emails, a visitor can repeat the one-line bio.

## Brand Personality
Sparse, confident, technical. Says less than it could. Reference lane: rauchg.com (Vercel dark minimal), leerob.com. Not a portfolio, not a resume, not a landing page.

## Anti-references
Template portfolio sites with hero gradients, skill bars, icon card grids, "Hire me" buttons, animated counters. Editorial magazine serif pages. Anything that looks like a startup landing page.

## Design Principles
- One column, left aligned, nothing centered.
- Lists over paragraphs: year, title, meta.
- Every claim verifiable; no adjectives about himself.
- Zero imagery is correct for this lane; a headshot can be added later as a small avatar.
- No motion beyond hover and focus states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, visible focus rings, keyboard-reachable links, respects prefers-color-scheme and prefers-reduced-motion, no text under 14px.
